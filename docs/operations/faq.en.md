# FAQ

Questions about how to proceed and how the material is positioned. For isolating execution errors, see [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md).

## Q. Which sample should I start with?

A. To go through Phase 1 onward without hardware, start from the [HA Demo Simulator](../hands-on/ha-demo-simulator.md). To run a single Phase 3 demo, `assistant-demo` ([LLM Planner hands-on](../hands-on/llm-planner.md)) is the shortest path because it starts with one command. With real hardware, start from [HUSKYLENS2](../hands-on/huskylens2.md) or the [USB webcam](../hands-on/webcam.md), depending on what you have.

## Q. Can I proceed without real devices or a smartphone?

A. Part 1 needs only a PC. The [HA Demo Simulator](../hands-on/ha-demo-simulator.md) runs without hardware, and the HUSKYLENS2 and USB webcam pages have a mock mode. The Part 2 hands-on pages that use the wallet need a smartphone.

## Q. Do I need real money or cryptocurrency?

A. No. The blockchain is a local Hardhat chain on your PC, and the ETH used for purchases is test ETH.

## Q. What is the difference between Part, Phase, and Stage?

A. Part is the learning order on this site, Phase is the development stage of the platform, and Stage is a sequence label for the hands-on pages that use VCs. See the [Hands-on overview](../hands-on/index.md#part-phase-stage).
