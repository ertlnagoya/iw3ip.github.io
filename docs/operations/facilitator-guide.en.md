# Facilitator Guide

!!! abstract "Where this page belongs"
    This page is in the "For Instructors" section for instructors and TAs. Planning the whole course is covered in the [Course Guide](../foundations/course-guide.md).

## Session goal

Participants should be able to complete the following.

1. Event generation (mock mode is enough)
2. Confirming registration as merchandise
3. Purchase
4. Data reception

See §1.3 of the [Hands-on overview](../hands-on/index.md) for the steps.

## Suggested flow (2 sessions, approx. 120 min)

This template runs from a device through trading on the marketplace to the phone check (§1.2–1.4 of the hands-on). It is different from the single-session plan in the [Course Guide](../foundations/course-guide.md) (HA Demo Simulator and HA × SSI Publisher). It does not fit in one 90-minute session, so split it over two. The time for each slot is based on the time required listed at the top of each page.

### 1. Introduction (approx. 10 min)

- Purpose of the data sharing platform
- Division of roles between on-chain and off-chain

### 2. Boot (approx. 30 min)

- Run the [Quickstart](../setup/quickstart.md) together
- Prioritize support for people who get stuck here

### 3. Hands-on (approx. 50 min)

- HUSKYLENS2 group: [hands-on/huskylens2.md](../hands-on/huskylens2.md) (approx. 45 min)
- Webcam group: [hands-on/webcam.md](../hands-on/webcam.md) (approx. 30 min)
- Groups that finish move on to the purchase and delivery in §1.3 of the [Hands-on overview](../hands-on/index.md)

### 4. Phone check (approx. 20 min)

- [hands-on/mobile-viewer.md](../hands-on/mobile-viewer.md)

### 5. Wrap-up (approx. 10 min)

- Share what worked and where people got stuck
- Present extension exercises

## Facilitator checklist

- [ ] All services started
- [ ] MetaMask test accounts ready
- [ ] Writing to `raw_data/output` verified
- [ ] Confirmed that a phone can open `http://<HOST_IP>:5173/`
