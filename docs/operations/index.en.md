# Support

Read this section when a step does not work as described during the exercises, or when you are unsure how to proceed. It is written mainly for participants and TAs.

- [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md): find the item closest to the symptom and work through the checks and actions in order. Also check the "Troubleshooting" part at the end of each hands-on page
- [FAQ](faq.md): questions about how to proceed and how the material is positioned, such as which sample to start with, whether you can proceed without hardware or a phone, and how Part, Phase and Stage differ

Course planning and session timing are covered for instructors and TAs under "For Instructors" ([Course Guide](../foundations/course-guide.md), [Facilitator Guide](facilitator-guide.md)). The mechanisms and the reasons behind the design are explained in the [Design](../design/index.md) section.
