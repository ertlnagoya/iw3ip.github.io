# References and Further Learning Materials

This page collects supplementary material to consult after finishing the learning in this site.
The basic understanding and hands-on can be completed with the explanations in this site alone.

There is no need to read everything at once.
Grasp the overall picture in this site first, then consult these materials when going deeper into social problems or technical specifications.

## Materials for Understanding Social Problems

### Privacy, safety, and community monitoring

The legal issues around security cameras with facial recognition are summarized in a document of the Personal Information Protection Commission [1]. A study on installing security cameras in harmony with privacy [2] and a study on large-scale installation of cameras in public space by local governments [3] help when thinking about the problems that come with installing and operating cameras.

### Missing persons, lost property, and public-interest information sharing

The number and breakdown of missing person reports can be checked in the statistics of the National Police Agency [4]. For lost property, the National Police Agency guides online applications [5] and reporting and searching [6].

## Materials for Learning the Technology

### Blockchain Basics

The Ethereum developer documentation [7] covers how blockchains, including smart contracts, work. The background of chaining blocks with hashes can be found in the Bitcoin paper [8].

### Hardhat

The Hardhat features used in this site are described in the official documentation [9], and the first steps in the getting-started guide [10].

### SSI / DID / VC

DIDs and VCs are specified by the W3C in DID Core [11] and the Verifiable Credentials Data Model 2.0 [12]. Standardization and implementation work on SSI can be followed at the Decentralized Identity Foundation [13].

### Implementation-Oriented Reading

The vulnerabilities to watch for when exposing an API are summarized in the OWASP API Security Top 10 [14], and the zero-trust approach of verifying every access in NIST SP 800-207 [15].

## Papers in This Site

The papers on IW3IP are listed on the [Publications](../publications.md) page.

## Recommended Usage

1. finish the foundation pages and hands-on in this site first
2. then read the social-problem materials to understand why this platform is needed
3. after that, study the technical materials for deeper detail on each component
4. finally use papers for research-level background
