# Publications

These are the papers on IW3IP. Use them for advanced follow-up study after the foundation pages and the hands-on pages of this site.

A decentralized IoT data distribution framework built on blockchain, with edge processing that lets data be processed flexibly on top of it, is proposed in [1]. Authentication of data providers and consumers with SSI (Self-Sovereign Identity), and condition-based search over metadata managed in PostgreSQL and IPFS, are proposed in [2]. The SSI-based hands-on pages of this site (such as [Authenticate the data provider (Consent VC / Stage 1)](hands-on/ha-ssi-wallet.md)) relate to the authentication method of [2]. [3] proposes a model, also built on blockchain, for sharing Personal Health Records (PHRs) and Electronic Medical Records (EMRs) with elliptic-curve cryptography, zero-knowledge proofs, and access control.
